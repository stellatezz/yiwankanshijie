Privacy Policy — short-factory

Last updated: 16 October 2026

short-factory is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channels 一碗看世界 (and the owner's other topic channels) to upload the owner's own videos to those channels. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the channel owner signs in to it.

What Google data the app uses

  • YouTube account access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube) — to upload videos to the owner's channel, set their title, description and tags, and change their privacy status (private, scheduled, public).
  • Channel name and ID — read once at sign-in, to confirm which channel was connected.

The app does not read, collect or store viewers' data, comments, subscribers, email, or any other account data.

How it is stored and shared

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent anywhere except to Google's servers when calling the YouTube Data API.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app has no servers, analytics or tracking.

short-factory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of the YouTube API is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored token. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/stellatezz/yiwankanshijie.
